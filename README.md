 🩺 Diabetes Risk Analysis & Prediction

📊 Power BI Mini Project

This project focuses on analyzing diabetes-related patient data to identify patterns, risk factors, and lifestyle-related insights using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**.

The raw dataset was first cleaned and prepared using **Microsoft Excel** and then imported into **Power BI** for data analysis, visualization, and interactive dashboard development.

---

 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze patient data and identify factors associated with diabetes risk.

The project aims to:

- Analyze diabetes risk levels among patients
- Identify major factors associated with high diabetes risk
- Analyze risk across different age groups
- Study the relationship between BMI and blood glucose
- Analyze blood pressure and HbA1c levels
- Study the impact of stress, sleep, and physical activity
- Analyze regional and residence-wise patterns
- Provide detailed patient-level information
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate meaningful insights and recommendations

---

🔍 Key Insights
The dashboard provides the following major observations:
The dataset contains approximately 50,000 patient records.
Around 73.19% of patients are classified as high risk in the dashboard.
Average BMI is approximately 30.92.
Average HbA1c is approximately 8.52.
Age, BMI, blood glucose and blood pressure are important variables for risk analysis.
Lower physical activity is associated with higher observed risk in the dataset.
Stress and sleep patterns provide additional lifestyle-related insights.
Regional analysis helps identify differences in patient distribution.
Note: These observations describe patterns in the dataset and do not establish medical causation or provide individual medical diagnoses.
